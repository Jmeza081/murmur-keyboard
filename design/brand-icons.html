Murmur icons

Six directions for the app icon, each shown the way Android will actually show it: under the circle, squircle and rounded-square launcher masks, small, and as a themed (monochrome) icon that takes its colors from the wallpaper. The last section is the wordmark.

  • 108 dp canvas, art inside the 72 dp safe zone
  • Reads at 48 px
  • One-color layer for themed icons
  • No text inside the icon
  • Orange = listening, cobalt = keyboard
