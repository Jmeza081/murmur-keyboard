Listening waveform: six directions

Each one sits in the real power-button sheet at about its real size, driven by the same simulated voice. Use the bar below to switch between speaking, quiet and loud. Every option uses the new level mapping already on your phone: a decibel scale where normal speech fills most of the height, with a fast rise and a softer fall.

Voice
In the app, reduced motion would swap scrolling, ripples and drifting lines for a still meter that only changes with your voice. Everything here can be drawn on one Android Canvas view at 60 fps. None needs OpenGL.
