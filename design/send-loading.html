Waiting on the transcript

After you tap the stop button, ElevenLabs can take a moment to send back the last words, and Claude cleanup adds more. Here are four ways to show that wait on the keyboard panel and the power-button sheet. Each one loops: listening, tap, waiting, inserted.

Theme

In every option the loader only appears if the wait passes 300 ms, so a fast reply never flashes a spinner. Past 3 s, a line offers the on-device model instead. Try "Fast" and "Slow" to see both.
