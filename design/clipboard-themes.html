Clipboard states and new themes

The clipboard gets a real empty state that points to your snippets, and smarter cards once you've copied things. Four new accents join the five you have, and every accent gets a third mode, Black, where the keys and background stay near-black but take on a hint of the accent.

Clipboard

Today the empty panel is two lines of grey text. The new one explains itself in one glance and fills the space with your snippets, so the panel is useful even before you copy anything.

Four new accents

Each one keeps at least 4.5:1 contrast with the white Enter and mic icons, like the current five. Shown in Light, Dark and the new Black mode.

Black mode, every accent

Black is a third mode next to Light and Dark, so it works with all nine accents. The background is about 6% accent over pure black, keys about 13%, and function keys about 9%. It stays dark enough for the Fold's OLED to save power, and each accent still reads at a glance.

Theme screen

The mode picker gains Black. The accent row grows to nine, with the new ones marked until you've opened the screen once.
