Saved logins, as a real bottom sheet

Tapping "+4" opens every saved login. Today the list is squeezed into the keyboard's own space. The proposal draws the same floating card as the power-button sheet, over the app, using the keyboard's full-height window. The phones below are open; Replay shows the tap.

How the proposed sheet behaves

  • Same card as the power-button sheet: inset 8 dp, rounded on every corner, handle, then the title and Done in your accent color.
  • Each login is a full-width row drawn by your password manager. Tapping one fills the field and closes the sheet.
  • Tapping the dimmed area, Done, or leaving the field closes it. The text field keeps focus the whole time, which autofill needs.
  • While it's open, the keyboard takes touches across the whole screen. When it closes, only the keys respond again.
