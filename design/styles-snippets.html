Writing styles and snippets

Two ideas. Writing styles change how dictation is written depending on the app you're in. Snippets are bits of text you use all the time, like your email or address, ready to drop into any field with one tap or a few spoken words.

Theme

01Writing styles

Murmur picks a style from the app you're typing in. Messages get Casual, email and docs get Polished, everything else gets Neutral, which is today's cleanup. A small pill in the strip shows the style. Tap it to switch, and Murmur remembers your choice for that app.

  • 1Casual: lowercase starts, no period at the end, slang stays lowercase (lol, idk), emoji welcome.
  • 2Neutral: proper sentences, your words untouched. What Light cleanup does now.
  • 3Polished: full punctuation and capitals, "gonna" becomes "going to", no filler. With Claude cleanup on, it also smooths the wording.

    Types into Messages

    On-device or Claude: Casual and Neutral are rules on the phone. Polished fixes punctuation on the phone too, and Claude cleanup does the rewording when you have it on. The style goes to Claude as one line in the prompt.

    02Snippets

    Save the text you type over and over. Murmur offers the right one when a field asks for it, keeps them all one tap away in a panel, and inserts them when you say their name while dictating.

    • 1Suggested by field: an email field shows your email as the first chip, and phone and address fields work the same way. It sits beside the password-manager chips.
    • 2Snippets panel: a toolbar button opens all your snippets, like the clipboard panel. Tap one to insert it.
    • 3By voice: say "insert my email" or "paste my address" while dictating, and the snippet goes in place of the words.

    Built on what's there: HeliBoard already has pinned clipboard items, so snippets can grow out of those, with a label, a voice phrase and the kind of field to suggest them in. They're stored on the phone and left out of backups, like your API keys. Tap the chips and cards in the mockups to try them.
