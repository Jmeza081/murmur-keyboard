Password chips: four ways to fix the strip

The chips above the keys come from your password manager (Google, Samsung Pass, 1Password). Murmur decides where they go and how big they are, but the password manager draws what's inside them. These options only use what Murmur can actually change.

What Murmur can and can't change

Murmur controls

  • Each chip's width and height (asked for before the chip is drawn)
  • The order, spacing, and the row around them (scrolling, paging, our own extra buttons)
  • Background shape and color, padding, icon spacing
  • Title and subtitle text color and size
  • How many suggestions to ask for (up to 6 today)

The password manager controls

  • The icon and the words ("Generated…", "slack.com", the account name)
  • Where the text cuts off with "…"
  • Whether it follows our style at all (some managers ignore it)
  • When each chip finishes drawing (they arrive in any order)
  • Effects on the chip itself (each is a separate window drawn on top, so no fades, masks, or animations across it)
