Android Keyboard: screens and flows

High-fidelity mockups for a personal Android keyboard with on-device dictation. Speech runs locally with Parakeet TDT v3, the model FluidVoice uses. Holding the power button starts dictation from any app. The companion app handles setup, the speech model, themes and the dictionary.

01

Keyboard

The dictation key stays at the right of the top strip in every state, so it is always one tap away. The strip shows suggestions while you type, and tools when the field is empty.

02

Dictation from the keyboard

Tapping the mic replaces the keys with a listening panel of the same height, so the app above doesn't jump. Speech never leaves the phone.

03

Emoji and clipboard

Each panel uses the same height as the keys. The strip above stays in place, with ABC at the left to return to typing.

04

Tablet: split keyboard

In landscape the keys split into two thumb zones. The dictation key moves to the centre, where either thumb reaches it. Drag the grips to change the gap.

05

Power-button dictation

The app is set as the phone's digital assistant, so holding the power button opens a listening sheet over any app. When you finish, the text goes where you were typing. If no field is focused, it goes to the clipboard.

06

Settings app

One app for setup and every option. The first screen is a checklist. After that, it opens to the settings list.

07

User flows

The two dictation paths share one engine. Times are targets to measure in the Phase 0 spike, not promises.

When something goes wrong

  • CancelThe X button, swiping the sheet down or switching apps discards the audio. Nothing is typed.
  • Model missingThe panel explains the 640 MB download and links to setup.
  • Microphone blockedThe panel shows Allow microphone, then returns to the keys.
  • Nothing heardAfter 8 s of silence: "Didn't catch that." Nothing is typed.

First run

  1. 1Install the APKSideloaded, signed with your key
  2. 2Turn on the keyboardOpens Settings › Keyboards
  3. 3Choose it as defaultSystem keyboard picker
  4. 4Download the model640 MB on Wi-Fi, checksum checked, in the background
  5. 5Set as digital assistantDefault apps › Digital assistant
  6. 6 · optionalAllow accessibilityLets power-button text reach any field
  7. 7Try itA practice field for the mic key and the power button
  • Motion: the panel swap and sheet use 200 ms ease-out. Key presses show feedback instantly with no animation, because they happen hundreds of times a day. The waveform stops moving when reduced motion is on.
  • Targets: keys are at least 44 dp tall, controls at least 44 dp, and text meets 4.5:1 contrast on both themes.
  • Privacy: audio is held in memory only and dropped after transcription. Clipboard history skips content marked sensitive by the source app.
