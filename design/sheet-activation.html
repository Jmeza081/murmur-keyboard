Power-button sheet: how it appears

Four ways the listening sheet could open. Press Play, or press and hold the power button on the phone's right edge (the lower of the two) for half a second, like the real thing. Release to close.

One Android limit shapes all four: apps aren't told about a power-button hold until Android decides it's long enough (about half a second). So nothing can animate during the hold. Each option plays the instant the sheet opens. Options B and C replay the hold as a quick flourish so the motion still points back to the button.
Timings follow the mockup's motion rules: entering uses a strong ease-out, exits are faster than entrances, and with reduced motion on, every option becomes a 150 ms fade. Every option can be built in the sheet with Android's standard animators. None needs anything new.
