Three ways in

Dictation wherever your thumb already is.

The mic key

It sits at the right of the suggestion strip in every state, so it's always one tap away. Tap it, talk, and tap Send.

The power button

Murmur is your digital assistant, so holding power opens a listening sheet over any app. It finishes when you pause.

Anywhere else

No field open? The text goes into the focused field through accessibility, or to your clipboard, pinned for an hour.

Dictation

Talk like a person. Get text like an editor wrote it.

Parakeet v3, NVIDIA's speech model, runs right on the phone. Tap the mic and the keys turn into a listening panel of the same height, so the app above never jumps.

Cleanup

The ums come out. Your words stay yours.

Light cleanup drops filler words and fixes spacing with simple rules, so it adds no delay and never rewrites what you meant. Add your own fillers, or turn it off. Want more? Claude cleanup, off unless you add your own Anthropic key, sends the finished text (never the audio) to Claude Sonnet, which fixes misheard words and follows "scratch that" or "actually, make it Tuesday".

Writing style

Casual in texts. Polished in email.

Murmur picks a style for each app: lowercase with no end period in chat apps, email-ready in Gmail and Docs, and as spoken everywhere else. Tap the style at the top of the panel to change it, and Murmur remembers your pick for that app. Say “heart emoji” to get ❤️, or “paste my address” to drop in a snippet you saved.

The keyboard

A full keyboard, not just a mic.

Built on HeliBoard, the open-source Android keyboard: swipe typing, smart suggestions, English and Spanish together, emoji, a clipboard you can pin, and a one-tap 1Password button.

Settings

One app. Every row tells you where it stands.

A five-step checklist gets you set up, and each step opens the right Android screen and ticks itself when you come back. After that, every option shows its current value, so you rarely need to open it.

Private by design

Your voice stays on your phone, unless you say otherwise.

Out of the box there's no account and no server. Murmur goes online once, to download the speech model, and after that dictation works in airplane mode. Two cloud options are there if you want them, both off until you add your own key.

  • Audio lives in memory only. It's dropped the moment your words are transcribed.
  • Passwords stay out of history. Clips marked sensitive by password managers are never saved.
  • Password manager, one tap away. 1Password suggestions show as chips in the strip, with a button to open the app.
  • ElevenLabs speech, if you choose it. With your key, recordings go to ElevenLabs for faster, live transcription. Offline or out of hours, the on-phone model takes over.
  • Claude cleanup, if you choose it. With your key, the finished text (never audio) goes to Claude to fix misheard words and follow "scratch that".
  • Snippets stay on this phone. Never offered in password fields, and left out of backups.
  • Open source. GPL-3.0, built on HeliBoard and sherpa-onnx. Read every line.
